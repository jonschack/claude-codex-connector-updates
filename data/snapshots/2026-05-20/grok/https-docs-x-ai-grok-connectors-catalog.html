Beyond the built-in office connectors, Grok offers a growing catalog of pre-configured connectors for popular third-party services. These connectors authenticate via OAuth and are ready to use with no additional setup.

To add a connector from the catalog:

  1. Go to grok.com/connectors.
  2. Click New Connector and browse or search the catalog.
  3. Select a connector and complete the OAuth sign-in flow.

The full catalog is available at grok.com/connectors. Below are connectors with dedicated setup guides:

ConnectorGuide
HubSpotHubSpot MCP Setup

More connectors are added regularly. If the service you need is not in the catalog, you can bring your own MCP server.
