# 02 — Positioning Angles (Ranked)

> 8 angles across both avatars. Each includes a one-line summary, the angle type, the avatar it
> hits hardest, and recommended use. Ranked 1 (strongest) to 8.

---

## Base Offer Summary
This event helps Phoenix small business owners get honest, specific answers about AI for their
business — in one free hour with practitioners who work inside local businesses every day.

---

## Angle 1 — Anti-Hype / Straight-Talk (WINNER)
**Type:** Contrarian / Trust
**Avatar:** Both (strongest with Skeptical Doer)

> "Every AI tool promises to change your business. One hour with people who've actually tried them
> in Phoenix businesses will tell you which ones are worth it — for you."

**Why it ranks first:** The overwhelming objection is AI fatigue and distrust of generic advice.
This angle pre-empts skepticism by *naming* it. It positions the meetup as the antidote to hype,
not more hype. "People who've actually tried them" is specific credibility, not a claim.

Variations:
- "Tired of AI advice that doesn't apply to your business? Bring your actual workflow."
- "Not a keynote. Not a vendor pitch. One room of operators getting real answers."
- "We cut through the noise for Phoenix business owners — one workflow, one hour, one honest answer."

---

## Angle 2 — Specific-to-Your-Business (Outcome)
**Type:** Outcome-specific / Niche
**Avatar:** Overwhelmed Operator

> "Most AI advice is built for someone else's business. This is for yours."

**Why it ranks second:** Specificity is the #1 thing the avatar craves. Generic content is the
problem they already have. This angle directly attacks the failure mode of every other resource
they've tried.

Variations:
- "Bring your actual process. Get an actual answer. For your business, not the generic one."
- "This isn't about AI in general. It's about what's worth doing for a business like yours."
- "30 minutes into the meetup, you'll have an answer that applies to what you actually do."

---

## Angle 3 — Time-Based (One Hour)
**Type:** Effort reduction / Speed
**Avatar:** Overwhelmed Operator

> "In the time you'd spend reading another AI article, you could get a real answer to your specific
> question from someone who's done it."

**Why it ranks third:** The primary "cost" is time. This angle collapses the time comparison with
alternatives (articles, YouTube, Google) and makes the one-hour commitment feel efficient rather
than heavy.

Variations:
- "One hour. One real question about your business. One concrete answer to take home."
- "Phoenix business owners: one free hour to go from AI-curious to AI-confident."
- "You'll spend less time here than you'll spend on the next AI article you don't finish."

---

## Angle 4 — Local Proof / Community
**Type:** Identity / Status
**Avatar:** Both

> "The people in this room run Phoenix businesses. They have the same problems you do."

**Why it ranks fourth:** Local specificity makes the event feel relevant in a way no national
webinar can. Peer credibility — other operators with skin in the game — is inherently trustworthy.

Variations:
- "Phoenix operators figuring out AI together. No gurus. No vendors. Just people doing the work."
- "The questions worth asking are too specific for a YouTube video. That's why we do this in person."
- "automationinterns.com is embedded in local businesses — not Silicon Valley. That's the difference."

---

## Angle 5 — Discovery / Hidden Opportunity
**Type:** Curiosity / Pain
**Avatar:** Overwhelmed Operator

> "You're probably doing something manually right now that could run itself. You just haven't found
> it yet."

**Why it ranks fifth:** Creates gentle urgency without fear-mongering. The hidden automation angle
surfaces latent desire — most operators *suspect* they're leaving time on the table, they just
haven't been shown where.

Variations:
- "Most businesses we talk to find at least one process they didn't realize could be automated."
- "Come in with your biggest time-waster. Leave knowing if there's a fix."
- "The process eating your Friday afternoons might have a one-week solution. Come find out."

---

## Angle 6 — Confidence / Not Behind
**Type:** Identity / Status
**Avatar:** Overwhelmed Operator

> "Leave feeling caught up — not overwhelmed by another list of tools you haven't tried."

**Why it ranks sixth:** The emotional outcome is often confidence and relief, not just information.
This angle speaks to the fear of falling behind and reframes the meetup as a "caught-up" moment.

Variations:
- "One hour to stop feeling behind on AI and start knowing exactly what to do."
- "You don't need to know every tool. You need to know which one matters for your business."
- "Walk out knowing where you stand — not just knowing more things to worry about."

---

## Angle 7 — Skeptic Welcome (Permission to Doubt)
**Type:** Identity / Trust
**Avatar:** Skeptical Doer

> "You don't have to believe AI will change your business. Just bring your biggest workflow
> headache and let's look at it honestly."

**Why it ranks seventh:** Removes the identity threat for skeptics. They don't have to "buy in" to
AI to attend. The bar is just: have a workflow problem. That's universally true.

Variations:
- "Skeptical about AI? Good. Bring that skepticism and a real workflow. We'll work with both."
- "No hype. No pitch. Just honest answers about what works and what doesn't for Phoenix businesses."
- "If AI isn't for your business, we'll tell you that too."

---

## Angle 8 — Mechanism / Format (How It Works)
**Type:** Mechanism
**Avatar:** Both (use when other angles have already created interest)

> "It's a live brainstorm — not a presentation. You bring a real workflow. We all work on it.
> You leave with answers."

**Why it ranks eighth:** The format is genuinely differentiated (interactive vs. lecture), but this
angle works best after trust is already established. Use to convert someone who's interested but
wants to know what they're walking into.

Variations:
- "Not a keynote. A working session. Bring your most annoying workflow and we'll dig into it live."
- "Real questions, answered live, in a room of Phoenix business owners who get it."
- "The format is simple: you bring the problem, the room helps solve it."

## Recommended Default Angle: #1 Anti-Hype / Straight-Talk

This is the one positioning angle that pre-empts the primary objection (AI is overhyped / not for
me) while also credentialing the source (practitioners in local businesses, not gurus). It works
for both avatars, across every channel, and it's differentiated from every other AI event. Use it
as the spine of the event title and event page headline.
