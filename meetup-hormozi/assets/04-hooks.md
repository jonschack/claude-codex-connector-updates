# 04 — Hooks & Headlines (20+)

> 20+ hooks across types, ranked within each category, tagged for use.
> Core message: Phoenix business owners → leave with a concrete AI answer for their business →
> in one free hour → without needing technical skills or wading through hype.

---

## Core Message Summary
- **WHO:** Phoenix small business owners / operators
- **RESULT:** A specific, honest answer about AI for their actual business + one concrete next move
- **PAIN:** AI noise / generic advice / not knowing what's real for their situation
- **SPEED:** One hour
- **EASE:** No prep, no tech background, free
- **Key objection:** "This won't be specific to my situation"
