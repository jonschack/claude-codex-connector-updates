## Usage Notes

**Sequence for first event:**
1. Post 1 (LinkedIn + X) — 2 weeks out
2. Post 4 (local FB groups) — 10 days out
3. Post 2 (LinkedIn) — 7 days out
4. Post 3 (X) — 5 days out
5. Post 5 (LinkedIn) — 3 days out
6. Post 6 (all channels) — day before
7. Post 7 — ask 2–3 connectors to share personally, 1 week out

**Do not:** Post all 7 on the same day. Spread across 2 weeks. Each post should feel like the
first mention, not a repeated blast.

**FB group etiquette:** In most local business FB groups, one post is fine. Don't cross-post to
too many groups with identical text — some groups flag this as spam. Slightly rephrase Post 4
for each group.

**DMs:** For personal invites, use the framework from Post 7. Keep it short. Don't over-explain.
"I thought you'd find this useful" closes more people than a full pitch.
